## Research themes [REQUIRED]

List 5-10 keywords that describe what the work actually is (taken from the user's own materials and confirmed by them). Specific is better than broad. Order matters — put the most defining themes first.

Example (good - specific, from an invented profile):
1. Single-cell RNA sequencing of tumour microenvironments
2. Graph neural networks for protein-protein interaction prediction
3. Causal inference with observational health records
4. Foundation models for biomedical text

Example (too broad - will retrieve too much noise):
1. AI
2. Health
3. Biology

- **Affiliated with a small business?** (yes — name it / no)
- **VA-affiliated?** (yes / no)
- **MD or MD/PhD?** (yes / no)
- **First faculty appointment date**: *(drives foundation early-career windows — e.g. Pew ≤3yr, Searle 1st–2nd yr, Sloan ≤6yr from PhD, BBRF YI ≤7yr)*
- **Citizenship for foundation purposes**: (US citizen / permanent resident / neither) *(several foundations and BWF CASI require citizen/PR; NSF CAREER and most others do not)*
