# Eligibility Cheatsheet

Quick reference for the eligibility codes and rules that come up most often. Use this to filter opportunities before ranking.

## NIH career-stage codes

- **ESI** (Early Stage Investigator) — within 10 years of terminal degree AND has not yet received a substantial NIH research grant. Many R01 review benefits apply (separate cluster, payline boost). Once you receive an R01, you lose ESI.
- **NI** (New Investigator) — has not yet received a substantial NIH research grant, but past the ESI 10-year window. Benefits are smaller than ESI.
- **K-eligible** — typically requires a recent terminal degree (within ~3-6 years for K99, ~6-10 years for K01/K08/K23) and a mentored research plan. Not for tenured PIs.
- **Established Investigator** — has held an R01 or equivalent. Required for K18 and some review-roles.

When the profile says "tenured" or "associate professor with R01," exclude:
- All K-series mentored awards (K01, K08, K22, K23, K25, K99/R00)
- F-series fellowships
- Most foundation early-career programs (Sloan, Searle, Pew, BWF CASI, Rita Allen)
- ESI-only NIH calls

When the profile says "early career, no R01 yet," include:
- ESI-eligible R01 calls (most R01 PARs/PAs)
- K-series matching their training stage
- Foundation early-career programs
- DP2 (New Innovator)

## Citizenship and visa

- **US citizen / Permanent resident only** — most NIH K-series, some DoD programs, some foundations (Searle, Pew). If profile flags non-US-citizen, drop these.
- **No citizenship requirement** — most R-series, most NSF, most ARPA-H, most foundations. International postdocs and faculty on H-1B / O-1 are typically eligible.
- **F31 fellowships** — US citizen/PR only. F31-Diversity has additional eligibility.

**Key takeaways for filtering:**
- A **non-US-citizen / non-PR** profile keeps: BBRF, Alzheimer's Association (esp. AARGA), BrightFocus, NSF CAREER, Klingenstein-Simons, Whitehall, HFSP, Sloan, most industry awards. Drop or flag-with-caveat: BWF CASI, AFAR/Glenn, anything saying "citizen or permanent resident".
- A **tenured / established PI** drops: all early-career foundation programs (Pew, Searle, Packard, Sloan, Rita Allen, Beckman, BBRF YI, Klingenstein-Simons, NSF CAREER). Keeps: McKnight MCD/TIN, BBRF Independent/Distinguished, BRF Scientific Innovations, Keck, HFSP, industry, DP1/DP2-equivalents.
- **Limited-submission** programs need an internal competition at the institution (e.g. UNC OoR) — surface them early even if the sponsor deadline looks far off.

## Institution type

- **Domestic institution** — most US federal awards. (state the user's institution; e.g. UNC qualifies).
- **Small Business Concern** — SBIR/STTR only. A university PI is not eligible as PI on these unless partnered with a qualifying small business.
- **501(c)(3)** — most foundations require this; a state university often qualifies via its affiliated foundation (UNC does, via the UNC Foundation).
- **Foreign components** — allowed on some NIH awards with restrictions; flagged as ineligible on others.

## Mechanism-specific eligibility flags

- **Clinical trial required / not allowed** — NIH FOAs explicitly state this. Computational/methods labs usually want "Clinical Trial Not Allowed" or "Optional" variants.
- **Human subjects required** — some methods labs can satisfy this with secondary data analysis (e.g., HCP, UK Biobank, ABCD, AoU); confirm in the profile.
- **Multi-PI allowed** — important for collaborative work. Most R01s allow it.
- **Resubmission limits** — A1 only for NIH; new submission allowed after.

## Common disqualifiers — auto-drop opportunities matching these unless profile says otherwise

| Pattern | Drop unless... |
|---|---|
| "Small Business Concern" | profile flags startup affiliation |
| "MD or MD/PhD required" | profile is MD or MD/PhD |
| "Foreign institution only" | always drop for US faculty |
| "Within 5 years of terminal degree" | profile is early-career |
| "Tenured faculty only" | profile is tenured |
| "Cancer center P30-affiliated" | the user's institution's cancer centre, if it has one (e.g. UNC Lineberger) — usually keep |
| "Clinical trial required" | profile flags clinical work |
| "VA-affiliated PI" | profile is VA-affiliated |

## Red flags worth surfacing rather than auto-dropping

These don't disqualify but the user should know:
- LOI required well before full proposal — note the LOI deadline prominently
- Limited submission (institution can only submit N) — flag for coordination with the institution's research office
- Cost-sharing required — flag the percentage
- Indirect cost cap below institutional rate — flag the rate
- Specific data-sharing or open-source requirements — flag if unusual
